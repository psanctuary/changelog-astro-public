🕸️ How a decentralized church can work

A mushroom has no headquarters. Underground, its mycelium links roots across a whole forest and moves water and nutrients to wherever they’re needed. The portal now asks what a church could look like if it grew the same way, in four threads:

  • Many hands, no single center: the member network, member-led clubs, and the two frontiers, Maine (Alana) and Pennsylvania (Christopher W).
  • Deciding together (a sneak peek): a network of the community’s own on open protocols like Lens and Farcaster, paired with a Psanctuary DAO.
  • Tokens of gratitude (a sneak peek): soulbound, non-transferable thank-yous from Christopher W’s gratitude-token project. One stays in the wallet it’s given to; it can’t be sold, sent on or traded, and it isn’t money. Still being tested; no dates.
  • A pledge, signed, not sent: here already (below).

Beside them, a small web of hyphae answers your pointer: nodes nearby light up and fresh threads reach for them, the way the spore field behind every page grows threads to the spores. Hover or focus a thread and its part of the web lights, so it works from the keyboard and on a phone too. The section headings now carry tiny spore prints, each card has a faint print in its corner, and the card edges turn slowly through the prism. All of it rests when you choose Still, in reading mode, with more contrast, or when your device asks for less motion.

🤝 A pledge of support

The new pledge page is a small, decentralized way to stand with the community. You read a short pledge, connect a crypto wallet (a browser wallet, a phone wallet by QR code, or an email sign-in) and sign it:

I support Psanctuary: a sincere community rooted in spiritual autonomy, peer support and communal care, and the tools its people are growing together, out in the open.

Signing is a message, not a transaction: no gas, no money, no contract. The signature is checked in your browser and then forgotten; nothing is sent, nothing is recorded, and the page never shows your wallet’s address. There are no counts: a signature proves a wallet signed, not that a unique person did, so no number will appear until pledges can be published as records anyone can check. A small tool below the pledge checks any pledge’s signature. A pledge isn’t membership; joining the church is its own step.

🧾 Receipts and taxes

The donate page ends with “Receipts and taxes”, in the church’s own words, quoted and linked: its legal notice describes Psanctuary Church as “a lawfully incorporated 508(c)(1)(a) Faith Based Organization”, and its donation page says “All donations to Psanctuary (as a 508(c)(1)(a)) are tax deductible”, adding “Please consult and confirm with your CPA regarding all tax related benefits for you personally.” For a receipt, email info@psanctuary.org with what you gave and when. It isn’t tax advice.

🍄 One door to the community

Community used to show two links to the member network on Mighty Networks, its front page and its plans. They turned out to be the same page for anyone not signed in, and both open the dashboard for members, so now there’s one: Join or sign in on Mighty Networks.

The Discord placeholder is gone too, and the window says why. Psanctuary once had a Discord, and Eric O closed it on purpose, so the community gathers in one place. A Discord may only ever come back as a help-desk back end most people would never see, unless a DAO or something new grows here. Time will tell.

📇 A profile and a contacts page

Psanctuary’s GitHub profile is now a full front page: a spore-print banner, every door (portal, AstroLog, changelog, library, map, ledger, donate, pledge, roadmap, sustainability, contacts, community), the two organizations and what each is for, and who’s who: Eric O co-founded Psanctuary with Courtney M and Athena; Alana leads the Maine frontier; Christopher W the Pennsylvania one. CONTACTS.md lists every way to reach the church, and Reach out links it instead of a placeholder.

🧭 Smaller things

  • Donate and the welcome guide are always there: in the footer, the navbar’s More menu and the Menu, on every page.
  • The bottom strip says it scrolls. On a phone, a soft fade and a small arrow show there’s more to the side.
  • These pages are planning to outlive any one person. The sustainability page and the roadmap now say who holds the keys today (Christopher W), and the steps to hand them to the church’s own people, including Psanctuary’s own wallet-connect project, one line in its settings.

Shared in Telegram, iMessage or Discord, it arrives with its own preview card.
