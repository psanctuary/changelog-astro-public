Where Psanctuary's GitHub home is headed, in rough order. What's already done is in the
[changelog](https://psanctuary.github.io/changelog-astro-public/changelog), and what keeps it all running is on the
[sustainability](https://psanctuary.github.io/changelog-astro-public/sustainability) page. This is also the repository's `ROADMAP.md`.

## ✅ Just landed

- **[How a decentralized church can work](https://psanctuary.github.io/#decentralized)** on the
  portal: a mycelium that answers your pointer, and four threads (many hands, deciding together,
  tokens of gratitude, the pledge). Spore-print marks and a slowly shifting prism, all resting in
  Still and reading mode.
- **A [pledge of support](https://psanctuary.github.io/pledge/)**, signed with a wallet: free,
  nothing sent, nothing recorded, no counts.
- **Receipts and taxes** on the [donate page](https://psanctuary.github.io/donate/), quoting the
  church's own legal notice and donation page, with receipts on request.
- **One link for the member network**: "Join or sign in on Mighty Networks". The Discord
  placeholder is gone (see below).
- **[CONTACTS.md](https://github.com/psanctuary/.github/blob/main/CONTACTS.md)**: every way to reach
  the church, linked from Reach out. And a full [organization profile](https://github.com/psanctuary).
- **Donate and the welcome guide everywhere**, and a bottom strip that shows it scrolls on phones.

## 🔜 Next

- **Give from a crypto wallet** on the donate page: the Connect button is there now, as a preview;
  giving opens once the church designates a treasury address of its own (never a placeholder).
- **Thanks you can claim yourself:** a contributor could add their name to the thank-you list by
  signing with a wallet, with no personal details involved.

## 🤝 Stewardship and succession

So these pages outlive any one person (today drasticstatic, Christopher W, owns or administers the
GitHub organization and holds the token that publishes this AstroLog). Details on the
[sustainability](https://psanctuary.github.io/changelog-astro-public/sustainability) page.

- [ ] At least two owners for the GitHub organization, from the church's own people
- [ ] Publishing through org-owned automation (a GitHub App, or an org bot account), not personal tokens
- [ ] A token register: every token and secret, its owner and its expiry date
- [ ] A renewal runbook, with a reminder a month before each expiry
- [ ] Hand-over steps written down: adding an owner, transferring repositories, domains and the wallet-connect project
- [ ] Exports and backups of the repositories and anything outside GitHub, on a schedule
- [ ] Wallet connect: Psanctuary's own Reown project (a one-line `org.js` change, `give.reownProjectId`), before any treasury address is published

## 🌌 Later, and still being explored

- **Tokens of gratitude.** Soulbound, non-transferable thank-yous from Christopher W's
  gratitude-token project: one stays in the wallet it's given to, can't be sold, sent on or traded,
  and isn't money. An optional, deeper step beyond a signed pledge. Still being tested; no dates.
- **A network of our own.** Profiles and posts on open protocols like Lens and Farcaster, so what
  members share stays theirs, paired with a Psanctuary DAO where members propose ideas and decide
  together. Privacy first. A direction, not a promise.
- **No Discord, on purpose.** Psanctuary once had a Discord; Eric O closed it so the community
  gathers in one place, on Mighty Networks. A Discord may only ever come back as a help-desk back end
  most people would never see, unless a DAO or something new grows here. Time will tell.
