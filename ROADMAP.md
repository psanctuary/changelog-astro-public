Where Psanctuary's GitHub home is headed, in rough order. What's already done is in the
[changelog](https://psanctuary.github.io/changelog-astro-public/changelog), and what keeps it all running is on the
[sustainability](https://psanctuary.github.io/changelog-astro-public/sustainability) page. This is also the repository's `ROADMAP.md`.

## ✅ Just landed

- **A full navbar on every page**, folding into a Menu on smaller screens, with an icon on every link.
- **One window for every way to reach the church**, and one for every place the community gathers.
- **A [map](https://psanctuary.github.io/changelog-astro-public/map)** of every place these pages lead, each with its own picture.
- **A [donate page](https://psanctuary.github.io/donate/)** with a thank-you list for those who'd like
  to be named.

## 🔜 Next

- **Give from a crypto wallet** on the donate page: one button for browser wallets, phone wallets
  and email sign-in. The slot is there now, empty on purpose until it's ready.
- **Thanks you can claim yourself:** a contributor could add their name to the thank-you list by
  signing with a wallet, with no personal details involved.
- **A contacts page** (`CONTACTS.md`) for this organization, so the placeholder in the Reach out
  window can go.

## 🌌 Later, and still being explored

- **A chat space for this playful side.** A Discord is a placeholder in the community window; nothing
  exists yet.
- **A network of our own.** Profiles and posts on open protocols like Lens and Farcaster, so what
  members share stays theirs, paired with a Psanctuary DAO where members propose ideas and decide
  together. Privacy first. A direction, not a promise.
